First Athletic Biotechnics release

Compact athlete's metronome.

A portable tempo instrument for repeated athletic actions: cadence, starts, plyometric contacts, shoulder rhythm, footwork, and controlled return-to-play progressions.

Purchase options
Tempo Range 30-240 BPM
Primary Cues Light, tone, vibration
Training Modes Steady, ladder, interval
Design Target Compact, durable, coachable

Designed role

Tempo you can place beside the rep.

AB-M1 is being designed as a compact training object, not a phone app. The goal is direct tempo control, clear feedback, and a device that earns its place on the track, floor, bench, or sideline.
